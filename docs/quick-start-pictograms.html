Hold PLAY for one second and it sleeps. A tap plays or pauses; only the long hold puts it away.

Recording overwrites. REC records over the selected track from the beginning. Recording is erasing, the same as tape.

MODE does nothing under two seconds. Deliberate — it stops a pocket press starting a pairing hunt.

Drawn to the board's real proportions — 99.50 × 63.50 mm, with the reel windows, lights and buttons in their actual places, so the pictures match the object in your hand. Buttons left to right: REC · PLAY · TRACK · MODE. Lights left to right: Bluetooth · REC · track 3 · track 2 · track 1. Prints on one sheet.
